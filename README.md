### `❯ whoami`

I'm Alf Emil — a **Machine Learning & Data Science** student at the **University of Copenhagen**. I enjoy turning ideas into working systems, with a particular interest in **AI, automation, developer tools, and software architecture**.

Alongside my studies, I'm building independent software products under **Alfemil**. My focus is on understanding how systems work, designing them thoughtfully, and shipping useful things.
